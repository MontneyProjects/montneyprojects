# Nightly add-on ideas

Ideas from the nightly MontneyPro check-up. Each run adds 3 new ones; don't repeat anything listed here.

## 2026-10-20

1. **Working-alone "I'm OK" button.** One big green button with a check-in timer (e.g. every 2 h, set by the foreman). If the worker doesn't tap it in time, the foreman gets a phone ping with the worker's last known GPS spot. Works offline: taps queue and send when signal comes back. Covers WorkSafeBC / Alberta OHS working-alone check-in rules.
2. **Parts & supplies request.** A grid of big picture buttons (gaskets, studs, rags, DEF, fuel, grease, etc.) with + / - counters. One tap "Send to shop" sends the list to the admin/shop with the truck number. No typing; the list saves on the phone until it sends.
3. **Lease road status.** At a lease, one tap picks Good / Rough / Chains needed / Closed, with GPS added automatically and an optional photo. Other crews dispatched to that lease see the latest status before they head out.

## 2026-10-23

1. **Near-miss / hazard snap.** One tap opens the camera, then pick one of four big icons (spill, gas/H2S, equipment, slip/trip). GPS and time are added automatically, it queues on the phone with no signal, and the office gets a ping. Hazards logged at a lease show at the top of the next FLHA for that lease.
2. **Big hour buttons on the field ticket.** Three large preset buttons (8, 10, 12 hrs) above the steppers, plus a "Same as yesterday" button that copies truck, client, job and crew so the worker only confirms. Cuts the ticket to two taps on a normal day.
3. **Truck walk-around photos.** Start-of-shift guided photos: front, driver side, back, passenger side, one tap each with a big outline showing which side is next. Photos are time- and GPS-stamped and saved to the truck's record, so damage disputes with clients or rental yards have proof of when it happened.

## 2026-10-24

1. **Gas monitor bump test.** Scan the QR sticker on the 4-head monitor, then tap one big button: Passed or Failed. It logs the monitor's serial, who did it, and the time. The FLHA shows a red "bump your monitor" bar until it's done that day, and a Failed test pings the office to swap the unit. Queues on the phone with no signal.
2. **Muster headcount.** In an alarm or evacuation, the foreman taps "Muster" and everyone on that job gets a full-screen alert with one giant "I'm at the muster point" button. The foreman sees a live list of who has tapped and who hasn't, with each person's last GPS spot. Works by SMS fallback when data is down.
3. **Talk-in scope of work.** On the field ticket, hold one big mic button and say what you did ("swapped two valves on the separator, pressure tested"). The phone turns it into text in the Scope box for the worker to check. No typing with gloves on, and tickets get real descriptions the client will accept.

## 2026-10-25

1. **Supervisor signs the ticket on the phone.** After the field ticket is filled in, one big "Get supervisor to sign" button opens a full-screen signature box. The client's supervisor signs with a finger and it's stamped onto the ticket with the time. Signed tickets are hard to dispute at billing time, and it works offline (the signature waits with the ticket).
2. **Tomorrow: Yes / No tap.** Every evening at about 6 pm each crew member gets a ping with three giant buttons: "Working tomorrow", "Off", "Sick". The office sees the list before sending dispatch, so nobody gets orders they can't fill and nobody is left out.
3. **"My hours look right" check before payday.** On cut-off day (15th and last day) each worker gets a ping showing their hours for the pay period by day, with two big buttons: "Looks right" or "Something's missing". "Something's missing" lets them tap the day that's wrong, so a lost or mistyped ticket is caught before payroll, not after.

## 2026-10-26

1. **On location / Off location buttons.** Two giant buttons on the home screen. Tapping one stamps the time and GPS spot (saved on the phone with no signal). That night's field ticket fills in start and finish times and hours from those stamps, so the worker only checks them and nobody has to remember when they got to the lease.
2. **Tickets wallet.** Each worker's safety tickets (H2S Alive, First Aid, CSO, Ground Disturbance, Class 1/3 licence) shown as big cards with the expiry date, saved on the phone so they open with no signal at a gate check. A card turns yellow 30 days before it expires and red when it has, and the office gets the same heads-up.
3. **Tool sign-out by QR.** Stick a QR sticker on each pricey tool (torque wrench, pump, gas detector, generator). Scan it and tap "Taking it" or "Returned". The office sees who has what and since when, so tools stop going missing between trucks. Taps queue on the phone until there's signal.
