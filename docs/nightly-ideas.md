# Nightly add-on ideas

Ideas from the nightly check-up. Each run adds 3 new ones; don't repeat what's here.

## 2026-10-29

1. **"I'm OK" lone-worker button.** One big button on the home screen when someone's working alone. Pick 1, 2 or 4 hours; if they don't tap "I'm OK" again before time's up, the office and their crew get a ping with their last known location. Saves on the phone and sends when there's signal.
2. **Snap & send incident / near miss.** Tap the button, take one photo, tap the type (Near miss, Spill, Damage, Injury). Time, truck and GPS fill in by themselves; it queues offline like the safety forms and pings the office when it goes out.
3. **One-tap "Need it" list.** A grid of the consumables the crew runs out of (rods, grinding discs, gas bottles, fuel, fittings). Tap the truck, tap what's low, done. The office gets one combined list per day instead of texts.
