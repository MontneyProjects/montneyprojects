Our health and safety program

Every job runs under our Health and Safety Manual. It covers 13 program elements, with more than 50 safe work practices and 27 safe job procedures written for the work we do.

  2. Hazard assessment and control
  3. Safe work practices

  6. Qualifications, orientation and training
  7. Preventative maintenance and inspections

  9. Emergency preparedness
  10. Incident reporting and investigation
  11. Company communications and statistics
